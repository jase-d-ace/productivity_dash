SMS Messaging Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Inkwell ("we", "us"), accessible at productivitydash-production.up.railway.app, handles information in connection with our SMS text messaging reminder service. It applies specifically to the mobile messaging program and to the information collected to operate it.

Information we collect

We collect the mobile phone number of the individual who has agreed to receive our text messages, along with a record of their consent. We do not collect mobile numbers from any other source.

How we use your information

The mobile number is used solely to send the recipient daily text reminders of their outstanding productivity tasks. We do not use it for any other purpose.

We will not share or sell your mobile information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text message opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, affiliates, or lead generators under any circumstances.

Message frequency

You will receive up to one (1) message per day as part of this reminder program. Message frequency may vary.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Any charges are billed by and payable to your mobile service provider. We are not responsible for any such charges.

Opting out and getting help

You can cancel the messaging program at any time by replying STOP. After you send STOP, we will send one confirmation message and then stop sending messages. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to andrada.jasonp@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
